R's random draws are replayed, so any difference would be arithmetic, not randomness. "yes" means the same float64 bits, so -0 isn't 0, except that any NaN matches any NaN (`same_values` in `tests/rfixtures.py`).

A row exactly as far from several centers goes to the lowest-numbered of them. With more than 16 centers R can take another of the tied centers ([compatibility.md](../compatibility.md#exact-distance-ties-in-create_dn)). "assignments" compares the port's own assignments with R's on every other row. "tie rows moved" counts the tied rows where the port's center is not R's, and each of them is checked to be the lowest-numbered of the tied centers, with R's center among them. The weights, centers and scales come from a run that goes on from R's assignments, as `tests/nuggets/test_create.py` does, so they compare the arithmetic and R's draws stay in step.
